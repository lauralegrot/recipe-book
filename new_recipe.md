# Arrabbiata pasta recipe


## Ingredients

- penne
- tomato sauce
- garlic cloves
- chili
- olive oil
- salt
- fresh parsley, optional
- pecorino Romano, optional

## Instructions

Fry garlic + chili in oil for 1 min. Add tomato and cook 10–15 min. Boil pasta al dente, drain, mix with sauce for 1–2 min. 
If you like, finish with parsley and a little grated Pecorino Romano.
