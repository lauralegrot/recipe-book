# Pasta boscaiola recipe


## Ingredients

- rigatoni
- pancetta
- peas
- mushrooms
- onions


## Instructions

1. Fry the onion in a little olive oil until soft.
2. Add the pancetta and cook until lightly crispy.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5–7 minutes.
4. Add the peas and cook for another 3–4 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Boil the rigatoni until al dente.
6. Drain, keeping a little pasta water, then toss the pasta with the sauce.
7. Add a splash of pasta water if needed and serve hot.
